## How to record (10 minutes)

1. A quiet room with soft things around (curtains, a bed, a sofa). No fan or AC noise.
2. Phone voice memo or QuickTime (File, New Audio Recording), phone a hand's width from your mouth, a little to the side.
3. Play `video/botfare-draft.mp4` muted. Start recording, wait 2 seconds, press play, and read each line when its subtitle appears.
4. A mistake? Pause, then say the whole sentence again. I cut the bad take.
5. Send me the file (m4a, mp3 or wav). I line up every scene, clean the sound and export the final.
