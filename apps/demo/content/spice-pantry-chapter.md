# The spice pantry, a chapter from our cookbook

The 20 spices in our cupboard, with one line on each.

- **Ajwain.** Small seeds that taste like strong thyme.
- **Allspice.** Tastes of clove, cinnamon and pepper at once.
- **Asafoetida.** A pinch in hot fat adds an onion-like depth.
- **Bay leaves.** For stocks and rice; take them out before serving.
- **Black mustard seeds.** Fry until they pop.
- **Black pepper.** Grind it fresh.
- **Cardamom.** Crush the green pods for rice and puddings.
- **Cayenne.** Heat, with little flavour.
- **Cinnamon sticks.** For rice and braises.
- **Cloves.** Strong, so two or three are enough.
- **Coriander seeds.** Citrusy when toasted and ground.
- **Cumin seeds.** Earthy, and the base of most tadkas.
- **Dried red chillies.** Fry them whole.
- **Fennel seeds.** Sweet and anise-like.
- **Fenugreek seeds.** Bitter if overcooked.
- **Kashmiri chilli powder.** Red colour, mild heat.
- **Nutmeg.** Grate it fresh.
- **Smoked paprika.** Smoky and sweet.
- **Star anise.** One is plenty.
- **Turmeric.** Cook it to lose the raw taste.

Buy whole spices in small amounts and keep them in airtight jars, out of the light.
