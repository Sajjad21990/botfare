# Five weeknight dinners from one shop

This plan feeds four people from Monday to Friday from one shop at the weekend. The dinners share onions, garlic, lemons, rice and spinach, so little goes to waste, and the most perishable things get used first. Tuesday's dal is a double batch, so you end the week with four portions in the freezer.

## The week

1. **Monday.** [One-pan lemon chicken and rice](/recipes/one-pan-lemon-chicken-rice), about 1 hour, mostly in the oven. Serve it with a salad of sliced cucumber and tomato dressed with lemon juice and olive oil.
2. **Tuesday.** [Weeknight red lentil dal](/recipes/weeknight-red-lentil-dal), doubled, with plain rice and a quick raita of grated cucumber, yogurt, salt and a pinch of ground cumin. About 45 minutes. Freeze half the dal before the tadka goes on.
3. **Wednesday.** [Crispy oven potatoes](/recipes/crispy-oven-potatoes) with fried eggs and garlicky spinach. About 1 hour 15 minutes, of which 20 minutes is hands-on.
4. **Thursday.** Chickpea, tomato and spinach stew with rice and yogurt. About 30 minutes. Cook twice the rice and chill half for Friday.
5. **Friday.** Egg fried rice with peas and spring onions, made with Thursday's rice. About 15 minutes.

## Wednesday's spinach

While the potatoes have their last turn in the oven, warm 1 tbsp oil in a large pan, add 2 sliced garlic cloves and cook until they start to colour. Add 400 g spinach a handful at a time, turning it until it wilts, and season with salt and a squeeze of lemon. Fry the eggs in the same pan once it is empty.

## Thursday's chickpea stew

Fry 1 chopped onion in 2 tbsp oil for 8 minutes until golden. Add 3 grated garlic cloves, 2 cm (¾ in) grated ginger, 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander and ½ tsp chilli flakes and stir for 1 minute. Add a 400 g tin of chopped tomatoes, 2 drained 400 g tins of chickpeas, 200 ml (¾ cup) water and 1 tsp salt. Simmer for 15 minutes, crushing a few chickpeas against the side of the pan to thicken the sauce. Stir in 200 g spinach until it wilts, add a squeeze of lemon and serve with rice and a spoon of yogurt.

Cook 600 g (3 cups) of uncooked rice tonight, twice what four people eat. Spread the extra half on a tray to cool, then refrigerate it within an hour. Rice that has spent a night in the fridge is drier and firmer, which lets it fry instead of steam.

## Friday's fried rice

Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large frying pan or wok over high heat. Scramble 4 beaten eggs until barely set and push them to one side. Add the cold rice, press it flat and leave it for 1 minute to toast before you stir. Add 300 g frozen peas and the sliced white parts of the spring onions, and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until the rice is hot all the way through. Season with 2 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, stir the egg back through and scatter with the green parts of the spring onions.

## Shopping list

Fruit and vegetables:

- 4 lemons
- 4 medium onions
- 2 heads of garlic
- 1 piece of ginger, about 8 cm (3 in)
- 2 green chillies
- 1.2 kg (about 2½ lb) floury potatoes
- 600 g spinach
- 2 cucumbers
- 4 tomatoes
- 1 bunch of spring onions
- 1 bunch of coriander and 1 bunch of parsley or dill

Meat, eggs and dairy:

- 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, about 1.2 kg (2½ lb)
- 12 eggs
- 500 g plain yogurt
- A small jar of ghee

Dry goods and tins:

- 500 g split red lentils
- 1.2 kg long-grain white rice
- 2 tins of chickpeas, 400 g each
- 2 tins of chopped tomatoes, 400 g each
- 600 ml (2½ cups) chicken stock
- 300 g frozen peas
- Soy sauce and toasted sesame oil

From the cupboard, check you have ground turmeric, ground cumin, ground coriander, cumin seeds, black mustard seeds, dried red chillies, chilli flakes, dried oregano, baking soda, neutral oil, olive oil, fine and flaky salt and black pepper.

## Order of work

Cook the chicken within two days of buying it, or freeze it the day you shop and thaw it overnight in the fridge on Sunday. If you have time on Sunday evening, salt it and leave it uncovered in the fridge, and it will brown better on Monday. Use the spinach by Thursday. On Tuesday, cool the second half of the dal in a shallow tub while you eat, then bag and freeze it as described in [freezing soups, stews and dal](/techniques/freezing-and-reheating).
